# Meow World — V0.999 Trial Learnings & V1 Product Concept

**Status:** Concept / Discussion Draft  
**Purpose:** Capture learnings from hands-on V0.999 use and ideas discussed for V1 UX/UI planning  
**Scope:** Documentation only — not an implementation authorization  
**Last updated:** 2026-10-12

---

## 1. Why this document exists

V0.999 gives the project owner a chance to use Meow World with real memories rather than judging the experience from specifications alone. After uploading some photos and reflecting on the experience, an important product question emerged:

> How can Meow World remain a personal home for memories while also helping pet lovers discover one another, share stories, and bring new people into the community?

This document records that learning and the resulting product direction. It separates observed trial learnings from proposed V1 behavior and decisions that still need review.

It does **not** state that the proposed Social, privacy, discovery, or external-sharing features already exist in V0.999.

## 2. V0.999 trial learnings

### 2.1 What was observed

- The project owner has begun using Meow World and uploading photos.
- Using the product with real memories reveals a need beyond data entry: the experience should help people preserve and revisit meaningful moments.
- A private timeline can be valuable on its own, but connecting pet owners may encourage people to return, share more stories, and invite others into the world.
- A purely private experience may not be enough to build a community of pet lovers; some social capabilities are likely necessary.

### 2.2 What this trial does not prove yet

This is an early, owner-led hands-on trial, not evidence from a broad user study. It does not yet prove:
- how often other owners would post or return;
- which discovery or interaction features they would use;
- whether public sharing improves retention or invites new members;
- which privacy controls people understand without assistance.

These are questions for future UX validation, not assumptions to encode as facts.

## 3. Product direction

### 3.1 One world, many connections

Meow World should be understood as **the world of pets and the community of people who love them**. It is more than a photo feed and does not need to copy every feature of a general-purpose social network.

Its distinctive center is the real life of a pet: a Home, the people who care for the pet, and a Life Journey that accumulates memories over time.

**Product principle:** Keep the memory at home; let the owner decide which stories travel.

### 3.2 Three connected spaces

1. **My Home — the personal home base**
   - A place to record and revisit the real life of one's pets.
   - Life Journey remains meaningful even when the owner shares nothing publicly.
   - Private memories must not be exposed just because another Journey is public.

2. **Meow World Discover — the pet-loving community**
   - A place to discover Public Life Journeys from other Homes.
   - A possible starting point for reactions, comments, and Home-to-Home relationships.
   - Discovery should support meaningful connections, not require users to compete for attention.

3. **Outside Social Platforms — the wider world**
   - Owners can share a story with people who do not yet use Meow World.
   - The shared content should communicate the story itself, not merely act as a teaser behind a forced sign-up.
   - A clear link should lead interested viewers back to the original Life Journey in Meow World.

These are conceptual destinations for V1 planning, not a statement about current navigation or implemented routes.

## 4. Life Journey visibility and sharing

Privacy should be defined at the level of an individual Life Journey, not inferred from the privacy of the entire Home.

### 4.1 Proposed visibility choices

| Concept | Intended audience | Intended behavior |
|---|---|---|
| **Private** | Owner and any explicitly authorized people under the applicable Home rules | Not discoverable publicly and not available through a public link |
| **Home / Selected People** | Authorized Home members or selected people | Available only to the audience explicitly granted access |
| **Public in Meow World** | Meow World visitors who are permitted to view public content | Discoverable inside Meow World and eligible for owner-initiated external sharing |

The exact labels, audience model, and whether all three choices are required in the first V1 slice remain to be decided during UX/UI design.

**Public must not mean Public Home.** A Home may contain many Journeys with different visibility settings. Making one Journey public must not publish other Journeys, pet records, member details, or private Home information.

### 4.2 Share the full story

The owner's intent is straightforward: when they choose to share a story, they want other people to see it as well.

The proposed external-sharing experience should therefore include:
- the selected photo or video, subject to the target platform's supported format;
- the caption/story text the owner chose to share;
- a clear link back to the original Life Journey in Meow World;
- the ability for viewers to comment on the native social platform, using that platform's own interaction system.

The viewer should be able to understand the shared story without being forced to open Meow World first.

### 4.3 Link back to the original world

The intended flow is:

```text
Real life with a pet
        ↓
Owner records a Life Journey in My Home
        ↓
Owner deliberately makes that Journey Public
        ↓
Owner shares its photo/video + caption to an outside platform
        ↓
People see and discuss the shared post on that platform
        ↓
A clear link opens the original Journey in Meow World
        ↓
Interested visitors can discover more public stories
        ↓
They may choose to join the Meow World community
```

Joining Meow World should be an invitation based on value, not a condition imposed before viewers can see content that the owner intentionally shared publicly.

### 4.4 Platform constraints

The intended experience must be designed around actual platform capabilities. External social platforms control their own previews, link behavior, publishing APIs, comments, and restrictions. Meow World cannot guarantee that clicking every image or every part of a caption will open an external URL in the same way on every platform.

Therefore, the design should:
- provide a visible, reliable Meow World link in the shared post wherever the platform permits;
- use supported share sheets, link previews, or publishing APIs rather than assuming identical behavior everywhere;
- treat comments made on external platforms as native to those platforms by default; do not promise automatic synchronization into Meow World;
- validate the behavior separately for each target platform before describing it as supported.

The goal remains: the image and story are visible on the external platform, and a clear path leads back to the original Life Journey.

## 5. Privacy and trust principles

### 5.1 Share by choice

- Private should be the safe default unless the owner deliberately chooses another visibility level.
- The owner should understand the audience before publishing.
- Public visibility of one Journey must not change the visibility of other Journeys.
- External sharing must be an explicit owner action, not an automatic consequence of uploading a photo.

### 5.2 Access must follow the current permission

If an owner changes a Public Journey to Private, Meow World must stop serving that Journey through public discovery and its public link according to the new permission. A previously copied URL must not bypass the access check.

External copies are a separate concern: content already published on another platform may remain there. The UI should communicate this before sharing and must not promise that changing visibility inside Meow World will delete every external copy.

### 5.3 Viewing and interaction are separate permissions

Being able to view a Public Journey does not automatically grant every interaction or account capability. V1 design must decide which actions require sign-in, such as commenting, following a Home, or saving a Journey.

### 5.4 Protect the surrounding Home

A public Journey should expose only the content intentionally included in that Journey. It must not inadvertently reveal:
- other private Journeys;
- private pet or household information;
- Home membership details;
- internal identifiers or private media URLs that bypass access controls.

These are security and privacy requirements to validate in implementation, not merely visual design details.

## 6. Social experience without losing Meow World's identity

The project does not need to become a general-purpose social network all at once.

A focused social direction could include:
- **Discover:** browse Public Life Journeys inside Meow World;
- **Meaningful interaction:** Like and comment where the owner and product rules allow it;
- **Home-to-Home connection:** explore a relationship model centered on Homes rather than making User-to-User friendship the only organizing concept;
- **External sharing:** help stories reach existing social platforms and provide a clear route back to Meow World.

These are candidates for V1 scope review. They are not all approved for implementation.

The product should prioritize memories, trust, and useful connections over engagement tricks, pressure to post publicly, or competition for likes.

## 7. Proposed V1 visitor experience

A visitor arriving from an external shared link should be able to:

1. Understand that they are viewing a Life Journey from Meow World.
2. View the public content that the owner chose to share, subject to the current permission.
3. Understand the pet/story context that is included in that public Journey.
4. Find a clear path to discover other public stories.
5. Choose whether to create an account to participate in community features.

V1 UX/UI should determine the precise signed-out experience, which interactions require authentication, and how the invitation to join is presented. Avoid blocking the public story behind a sign-up wall if the owner deliberately shared it for people to see.

## 8. V1 decision checklist

Before implementation begins, review and decide the following:

### Product and navigation
- [ ] Is **My Home** the default entry experience, with Discover as a separate destination?
- [ ] What is the smallest useful Discover experience for V1?
- [ ] What does a visitor see when arriving from an external shared link?

### Privacy and permissions
- [ ] Which visibility options ship in the first V1 slice: Private, Home/Selected People, Public?
- [ ] Who can see a Journey at each visibility level?
- [ ] Can a Public Journey be viewed without an account?
- [ ] Which interactions require authentication?
- [ ] How are permissions enforced when visibility changes or a link is reused?
- [ ] What warning appears before a story is shared externally?

### External sharing
- [ ] Which platform(s) should be tested first?
- [ ] Is sharing initiated through the native share sheet, copied link, or a supported publishing integration?
- [ ] How are photo/video, caption, and the Meow World return link presented on each target platform?
- [ ] Are external comments intentionally kept on their original platform?
- [ ] What behavior is guaranteed, and what depends on each platform?

### Community and safety
- [ ] What Like, Comment, and Home-to-Home behavior is needed for the first vertical slice?
- [ ] How can owners hide, edit, or delete their own content?
- [ ] What reporting, blocking, and moderation controls are necessary before public discovery expands?

### Evidence and acceptance
- [ ] Can we prove that private Journeys are absent from public discovery and inaccessible through public links?
- [ ] Can we prove that changing a Journey to Private invalidates public access on the next request?
- [ ] Can a signed-out visitor view an intentionally public Journey without seeing unrelated private data?
- [ ] Can a share preview be tested end-to-end on each selected external platform?
- [ ] Have we observed a real user complete the flow without guidance?

## 9. Suggested implementation discipline

When V1 is approved, build and validate a narrow vertical slice rather than implementing the whole social vision at once:

1. **Reconcile:** inspect the current V0.999 data model, permissions, media storage, and existing interaction behavior.
2. **UX/UI design:** map the owner and visitor journeys; review privacy states and error/empty states.
3. **Foundation:** define data ownership, visibility, authorization, and public-link rules.
4. **Vertical Slice:** implement one approved flow from publishing a Public Journey to viewing it through a shared link.
5. **Evidence:** verify database permissions, public access behavior, external preview behavior, and regression tests before expanding scope.

Do not infer feature completion from a design mockup or document. Record implementation status only after code, runtime behavior, and permission evidence have been checked.

## 10. Scope boundary

This document is a **post-trial concept and V1 planning input**.

It does not authorize:
- implementing a complete social network;
- enabling public access to all Homes or Journeys;
- automatic external publishing;
- synchronization of comments across platforms;
- broad recommendation algorithms or engagement optimization;
- changes to production permissions without review and verification.

The next step is to review this document with the project owner, agree on the V1 UX/UI scope, and then reconcile that scope against the current code and database before implementation.

---

## Decision summary

**Direction to preserve:** Meow World is the world of pets and the community of people who love them. My Home remains a place for real-life memories. Owners choose which Life Journeys to share. Public Journeys can be discovered inside Meow World and, when deliberately shared, show their photo/video and story on an external social platform with a clear link back to the original Journey.

**Guiding principle:** **Private by default. Share by choice. Let the story travel; keep the owner in control.**
